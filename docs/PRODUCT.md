# Product Specification & Principles (PRODUCT.md)

## Vision & North-Star
A luxury, cloud-native multi-tenant hotel operations SaaS platform that bridges high-end guest hospitality with frictionless real-time kitchen, front desk, and housekeeping operations.

## Personas & User Journeys
1. **The Resort Guest (PWA / Mobile)**:
   - Enters room, scans stylish QR code on bedside stand.
   - Instantly lands in active stay session without app download or complex sign-up.
   - Browses curated digital menu, adds special culinary requests, orders room dining or amenities.
   - Tracks live delivery status with real-time status changes.
   - Reviews folio balance and pays/checks out digitally.
2. **The Kitchen Chef & Runner (Kitchen Workspace)**:
   - High-contrast, large touch-target workspace designed for kitchen tablets.
   - Audible bell chime when new orders drop.
   - Filter by preparation status: Pending -> Accepted -> In Preparation -> Ready -> Delivered.
3. **The Front Desk & Housekeeping Team**:
   - Live room boards, guest luggage assistance, towel/amenity requests with target SLA countdowns.
4. **The General Manager**:
   - Executive dashboard displaying occupancy, average response time, order volumes, revenue, and active escalations.
5. **The Platform Owner**:
   - Multi-tenant tenant control plane: hotel onboarding, subscription limits, usage metrics, and audit log inspection.
